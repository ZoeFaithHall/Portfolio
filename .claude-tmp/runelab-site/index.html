The standard

Six pillars. One reading.

Pillar / 01

Power

Who holds real decision-making power, and is it written down?

Pillar / 02

Pay

Is pay set by a formula anyone can read, with no gap?

Pillar / 03

Time

Can people do full work in sustainable hours?

Pillar / 04

Care

Do leave and caregiving support work for everyone who needs them?

Pillar / 05

Accountability

When harm is reported, is it resolved on time, with consequences?

Pillar / 06

Safety

Do people, women especially, report feeling safe, and do the numbers agree?

Accessibility runs through every pillar, and the platform meets WCAG 2.2 AA. Every mechanism applies to every employee. Certification is independent: whoever helps a company implement never certifies it.

How it works

Start small. Grow with the results.

Each step costs little until your own data shows it working. You move on only when the numbers say so.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Free

    Share 24 months of HR data. See your turnover cost in dollars and where you stand on each pillar.

  2. 02

    Measure

    A baseline, a pulse survey and harm-report tracking, so you can see Power, Accountability and Safety.

  3. 03

    Pilot

    A 32-hour week on one or two teams, measured against the rest of the company. You roll out only if your own results hold up.

  4. 04

    Roll out

    All six pillars, company-wide, once your own results hold up.

  5. 05

    Certify

    Independent review

    Reviewed by a separate body, published, and renewed every two years. Each year you see where you stand against peers.
